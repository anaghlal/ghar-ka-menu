How to use Ghar Ka Menu

A shared family meal planner. It suggests meals for the next 7 days that match everyone's calorie and protein targets. You and Abhilasha can change anything; changes appear on both phones.

A typical day

  1. Evening (5 pm): open Today, scroll to “Prep this evening”, and tick off tasks for tomorrow (soak rajma, set curd, marinate, chop vegetables for lunchboxes).
  2. Next morning: open the day, cook what the cards say. The line “Cook 4 katori for the family” and the side chips (e.g. Phulka · 14) tell you the total quantity.
  3. Something changed? Tap Swap to pick another dish, or Skip if you ate out.
  4. After the meal: tap Cooked, then Rate to record how each person liked it. This makes future plans better.
  5. Optional: Share on WhatsApp to send the day's menu to the family group.

Dish name (tap it)
Opens the full recipe: ingredients, method, night-before steps, and “Your kitchen” notes (e.g. use the microwave's Convection or Grill mode instead of an oven).
Swap
Shows other dishes that fit this meal and today's rules (veg day, weekly fish/chicken limits, time limit, lunchbox). Tap one to replace the dish; portions and sides are recalculated for everyone. You can also search, or type a wish like “something light with paneer” and tap Ask Claude (needs an API key in Settings). A swapped dish is automatically locked so it won't be changed by re-planning.
Cooked
Tells the app this meal was actually made. What it does:
  • Shows a green Cooked tag on the card and a ✓ in the Week view, so the other parent knows it's done.
  • Freezes the meal – “Re-plan” will never change a cooked meal.
  • Reminds you to Rate it.
Tap Undo cooked if you marked it by mistake.
Skip
Use when the meal won't be made (eating out, guests, leftovers). What it does:
  • Removes it from the day's calorie/protein totals.
  • Removes its ingredients from the Shopping list.
  • The card fades and shows a red Skipped tag.
Tap Undo skip to bring it back. If you'd rather cook something else, use Swap instead.
Rate
For each person choose Loved, OK or Didn't work. All dishes start neutral.
  • Loved → the dish comes up more often.
  • Didn't work → it comes up less often.
  • If everyone marks Didn't work, it is never suggested again (you can still choose it from the Recipes tab).
You can rate the kids on their behalf. The number on the button, e.g. Rate (3), is how many people have rated it.
Edit portions
Change one person's plate with + / − (e.g. 3 phulka instead of 4, or ½ katori less rice). Their calories and protein update straight away, and the shopping list follows. Use it when someone is extra hungry or not eating much.
Lock / Unlock
A locked meal (🔒 on the card) is kept as it is when you tap “Re-plan”. Use it for meals you've decided on, e.g. Sunday biryani.
Portions for each person (tap to expand)
What each person should eat: the dish, the sides, and their calories and protein. Red notes are personal adjustments, e.g. “max 1 whole egg; rest egg whites” for Anagh, or “serve before adding ghee”.
Tags
Lunchbox packable for school/office lunch · Convection/Grill uses that microwave mode · Book recipe from one of the recipe books (others are everyday dishes) · by Name who last changed it.

Today screen

Date strip
Tap a day to see it. A green underline marks the vegetarian day (Thursday).
Totals (top card)
Each person's calories and protein for the day vs. their target. Bars turn green when calories are within 10% of target and protein is at least 90% of target. Skipped meals are not counted.
Prep this evening
Checklist for tomorrow's meals. Ticks are shared, so both phones see what's done.
Re-plan unlocked meals
Gives fresh suggestions for this day. Locked and cooked meals stay.
Share on WhatsApp
Sends this day's menu as a message (for Son 1 and the family group).
The next 7 days are planned automatically when you open the app. Days already planned aren't changed unless you re-plan them.

Week

An overview of the next 7 days. Tap a day to open it. Re-plan unlocked refreshes the whole week but keeps locked and cooked meals.

Shopping

What to buy for the next 1–7 days (choose at the top), starting from the selected day, grouped by category. Tick items as you buy them; ticks show on both phones. Pantry check lists spices and small items to check you have. Quantities are approximate.

Ticks belong to the start date. If you pick a different start day, you get a fresh list.

Recipes

Search & filters
Search by dish or ingredient. Filters: everyday (home staples), book (your recipe books), custom (added by you), or by meal.
Recipe page
Full recipe, nutrition per portion, what it goes with, ratings, and Use for breakfast/lunch/… to put it on the selected day.
Add recipe
Paste a recipe (WhatsApp, website, or family style) and tap Structure with Claude. Claude fills in the meal type, sides, nutrition and steps; check it and save. Without an API key, fill in the basics yourself. New recipes are shared with the family and can appear in plans.

FAQ

I swapped a dish but it changed back.
Both phones edited the same meal at nearly the same time; the later edit wins. Swap again. It stays, because swapped meals are locked.
Why so many phulkas/katoris for Son 1?
Portions are sized to each person's daily target. Use Edit portions to adjust for one meal. If it's always too much, the targets can be changed on the laptop (family_input.json).
A dish uses an oven / air fryer.
Open the recipe: the Your kitchen box says which microwave mode (Convection, Grill, Combi, Slim Fry) or gas-hob method to use instead.
Why no fish this week?
Weekly limits allow fish once a week. Change it in Settings → Weekly limits.
Sweets and desserts don't appear.
The family plan avoids added sugar, because Anagh's diet is zero-sugar.
The app works without internet?
Yes, with what was last synced. Changes are saved on the phone and sent to Drive when you're back online.

Nutrition values come from the recipe books where given, otherwise standard Indian food tables; targets are general estimates, not medical advice.
